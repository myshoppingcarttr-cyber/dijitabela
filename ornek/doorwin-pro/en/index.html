How a job moves through the app

The order is the same as in your workshop: open the job, draw it, price it. These are the app’s own screens.

  1. Every job has its number

    The list shows job number, system and customer side by side: 108, UPVC, Northshore Studio. Customers have their own tab.

  2. Pick the opening, type the size

    Pick mullions, sashes, openings and special shapes from the toolbar. A 2400 width splits into 1200 + 1200 and the dimension chain stays on the drawing.

  3. Take-off and price in one table

    Frame 42 m, sash 27.2 m, double glass 17.74 m². Each line uses your unit price; total, profit and total with profit sit at the bottom.

  4. Automatic and thresholdless doors

    A sensor sliding door gets its own line in the estimate. Version 1.1.0 improves thresholdless doors and adds compatibility checks for casings, sills and automatic doors.

  5. A new phone doesn’t lose your work

    Projects, drawings, customers, business details and prices go into one file and import on the new device. Nothing uploads automatically; the file stays with you.

Frequently asked questions

What is DoorWin Pro?

A mobile app that brings window and door drawing, material take-offs and pricing together for PVC/uPVC and aluminium fabricators, installers and sales teams.

#what-is-doorwin-pro

Which devices does it run on?

According to the App Store listing: iPhone and iPad on iOS 15.6 or later, Macs with M1 on macOS 12.5 or later, and Apple Vision. Android availability should be confirmed on Google Play.

#which-devices-does-it-run-on

How does it calculate prices?

It works out profile lengths, glass and panel areas and accessories from your dimensions and applies your own price list; you set currency, tax and margin. Review the result before sending.

#how-does-it-calculate-prices

How much does it cost?

The download is free; in-app Premium is listed at $7.99 monthly or $79.99 yearly on the US App Store. Prices may vary by country.
