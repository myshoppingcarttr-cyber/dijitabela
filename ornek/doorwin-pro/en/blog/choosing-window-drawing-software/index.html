5 questions when choosing window drawing software

Do you need cutting optimisation, or a quote at the customer’s side? Choosing software starts there, then four more questions.

Author: DoorWin Editor (sample author) · Published: 23 October 2026

The first split: for production planning and cutting optimisation, look at desktop software. For fast drawing and quoting on site, look at a mobile tool. They do different jobs, and many firms use both.

Five questions before a demo

  1. Where do I measure, and where do I quote? If quotes happen on site, desktop software won’t fix the delay.
  2. Can I use my own price list? Without separate prices for series, colour and glass the numbers won’t hold.
  3. Which systems and shapes can it draw? Try your hardest job: arches, thresholdless or automatic doors.
  4. Can the customer read the output? Technical drawing, opening direction, with or without prices.
  5. Does my data come with me? How do projects, customers and prices move to a new device?

When you test

Use a real job from last week, not the demo project. Compare the result with your own calculation and ask about any gap. Read the Lite and Pro comparison with this in mind.

Questions and answers

Can a mobile app replace production software?

Not always. Cutting plans and machine links need separate production software.

Is a free trial enough?

If you can test on a real job, it reveals most needs.
