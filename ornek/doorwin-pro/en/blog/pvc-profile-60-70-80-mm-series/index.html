How to read 60, 70 and 80 mm PVC profile series

The number is the profile’s depth, not its quality. Quoting errors often start here: selling 80 series at the 70 series price.

Author: DoorWin Editor (sample author) · Published: 5 October 2026

In short: 60, 70 and 80 give the profile’s depth in millimetres. Chambers and insulation values can’t be read from that number; check the system catalogue.

Four catalogue lines to check

  • Uf value: heat transfer through the profile. Lower is better.
  • Glazing rebate: the thickest glass unit it takes. Check this first for triple glazing.
  • Reinforcement section: decides deflection on large sashes.
  • Number of gaskets: affects air and water tightness.

Keep series apart in your price list

A 1500×1500 double sash has 6 metres of frame. Change the series and the metres stay; the price per metre doesn’t. A workshop with one list pays the difference from its own margin when it sells 80 series at the 70 price.

What goes on the quote

Brand, series, colour and glass unit on separate lines. A quote that only says “PVC window” can’t be compared on anything but price. For the metre calculation itself, read the pricing article.

Questions and answers

Is the chamber count enough on its own?

No. Chamber count alone does not give the insulation result; glazing and installation quality matter too.

Does every series take the same glass?

No. Accepted glass thickness depends on the manufacturer’s data for each series.
