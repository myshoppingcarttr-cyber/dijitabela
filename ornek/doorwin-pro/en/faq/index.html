Frequently asked questions

Short answers on the product, costing and buying.

Product

What is DoorWin Pro?

A mobile app that brings window and door drawing, material take-offs and pricing together for PVC/uPVC and aluminium fabricators, installers and sales teams.

#what-is-doorwin-pro

Which devices does it run on?

According to the App Store listing: iPhone and iPad on iOS 15.6 or later, Macs with M1 on macOS 12.5 or later, and Apple Vision. Android availability should be confirmed on Google Play.

#which-devices-does-it-run-on

Which languages does it support?

The App Store listing shows 26 languages, including Turkish, English, German, Arabic, Russian, French, Spanish and Hindi.

#which-languages-does-it-support

Which window and door types can I draw?

The toolbar has mullions, fixed, opening and sliding parts plus special shapes. The store screenshots also show an automatic sensor sliding door.

#which-window-and-door-types-can-i-draw

Costing and quotes

How does it calculate prices?

It works out profile lengths, glass and panel areas and accessories from your dimensions and applies your own price list; you set currency, tax and margin. Review the result before sending.

#how-does-it-calculate-prices

In which formats can I share a quote?

You can export the quote with technical drawings as PDF or PNG and share it.

#in-which-formats-can-i-share-a-quote

What happens to my data when I change devices?

You can export projects, drawings, customers and prices to a file and restore them on the new device. Sign-in and Premium status are not in the backup; sign in again.

#what-happens-to-my-data-when-i-change-devices

Can I change the currency and tax?

Yes. Currency, tax rate and profit margin are your settings, so you can quote abroad in the right currency.

#can-i-change-the-currency-and-tax

Is my backup uploaded automatically?

No. According to the app, the backup stays on your device or the location you choose and is not uploaded automatically. Share the file and import it on the new device.

#is-my-backup-uploaded-automatically

Buying

How much does it cost?

The download is free; in-app Premium is listed at $7.99 monthly or $79.99 yearly on the US App Store. Prices may vary by country.

#how-much-does-it-cost

I don’t want a subscription. Is there an option?

The DoorWin app with a one-time payment has no monthly or yearly subscription. The comparison page shows both apps line by line.

#i-don-t-want-a-subscription-is-there-an-option

Can I get a refund if I’m not satisfied?

The current site states you can cancel or request a refund within 14 days through the store you downloaded from. The process follows that store’s rules.

#can-i-get-a-refund-if-i-m-not-satisfied

Is DoorWin Pro available on Android?

The verified Google Play link belongs to the one-time DoorWin app. Once Pro’s Android link is confirmed, it is added in the panel and the button updates.

#is-doorwin-pro-available-on-android
